# ClauseVerify AI — B2B Lead Sourcing & Prospecting Guide

---

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Industry:** B2B SaaS, FinTech, HealthTech, CyberSecurity, Cloud Infrastructure, HR Tech.
- **Company Size:** 25 to 500 employees (Seed to Series C).
  - *Why this size?* They sell to enterprise customers who demand rigorous 100-question security assessments, but they don't have a 20-person legal department, so their engineers/founders are drowning in paperwork.
- **Geography:** United States, Canada, United Kingdom, European Union.

---

## 2. Job Titles to Filter in Apollo / LinkedIn Sales Navigator

### Primary Buyers (Economic Decision Makers):
- `Head of Compliance`
- `Chief Compliance Officer`
- `General Counsel`
- `VP of Legal` / `Legal Director`
- `Director of Information Security` / `CISO`

### Secondary Champions (The People Who Feel the Pain Daily):
- `VP of Sales Operations`
- `Lead Sales Engineer` / `Solutions Architect` (they usually get stuck filling security questionnaires)
- `Risk & Compliance Manager`
- `Security GRC Analyst`

---

## 3. Daily Outbound Cadence for $10k MRR:
- **Daily Volume:** 40 targeted emails + 25 personalized LinkedIn connection requests.
- **Expected Benchmark Metrics:**
  - Open Rate: 45%–55%
  - Reply Rate: 6%–10% (~3 to 5 warm replies per day)
  - Demo / Trojan Horse conversion: 1 to 2 pilots started per day
  - Paid Subscription Close: 3 to 5 new paying customers per week (\$1,500–\$3,500 MRR added weekly).
