## 2. Maker's First Comment (Post on Launch Morning)

> Hey Product Hunt community! 👋
> 
> I built **ClauseVerify AI** after watching founders, compliance officers, and sales reps spend days drowning in 60-page PDF contracts and 100-question vendor security questionnaires.
> 
> The biggest issue with using generic AI like ChatGPT for contracts is **hallucination risk**: if an AI misquotes a liability limit or SLA credit by one sentence, you could face legal disaster.
> 
> **How ClauseVerify AI fixes this:**
> 1. **Zero-Hallucination Anchoring:** Every answer cites the exact verbatim sentence from your document. If a clause doesn't exist, it explicitly tells you.
> 2. **Split-Screen Visual Highlighting:** When you click any citation, the right-hand PDF viewer jumps to the exact page and paints a pulsing yellow highlight box over the clause.
> 3. **Questionnaire Auto-Filler:** Upload a 50-question enterprise security checklist and auto-populate all answers with proof citations in 10 seconds.
> 4. **SOC 2 Audit Ledger:** Every query and cited snippet is logged to an immutable audit trail for external auditors.
